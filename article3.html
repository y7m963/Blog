Pattaya is more than neon lights and beach chairs. The numbers tell a story of a city that reinvented itself – from a quiet fishing village to one of Thailand’s most visited destinations.

Whether you come for the islands, the street food, or the buzzing night markets, the data above can help you plan smarter: pick the right month, focus on what matters most to you, and leave room for surprise.
